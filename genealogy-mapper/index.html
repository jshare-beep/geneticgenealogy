Where do the trails meet?

Genealogy Mapper plots separate paper-trail ancestries - each its own color - on a shared map and timeline, so you can spot the times and places where people from different family trees could plausibly have crossed paths.

1. Add a family tree One color per paper trail you're following - split it further into more family trees any time.
2. Add people Upload a GEDCOM file, paste rows from a spreadsheet, or fill people in by hand - birth, death, and any other places lived.
3. Watch the map Play through time and see when two different family trees' dots land in the same place at once - a possible lead worth checking.
